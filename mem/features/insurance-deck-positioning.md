The `/insurance` deck (`src/pages/InsuranceDeck.tsx`) is the working brief for GCC insurance leadership.

Positioning: pure **Snyder PULL**. Do not open with our category, our acronyms, or our infra metaphors. Open with the buyer's Friday-morning reality, real third-party pressure, and four concrete use cases. Only slide 10 lifts the hood, and only if asked.

10-slide structure (do not reorder without re-running the council):
1. Cover · Five questions on the desk of every insurance leader this quarter
2. Five conversations — composite quotes from Head of Claims, CRO, Chief Underwriter, Head of Customer, Head of SIU
3. Why now, why structural — Lokken v. UnitedHealth (Mar 2026), Nippon Life v. OpenAI (Mar 2026), Tawuniya InsurAI / Arabic hallucination, CBUAE 2025-2026
4. Use case 1 · Claims-exception decisions with a defensible rationale (Lokken response)
5. Use case 2 · Underwriting standard propagation after a market shock (Apr 2024 floods)
6. Use case 3 · Fraud / SIU senior judgment capture
7. Use case 4 · Arabic / dialect-safe customer service
8. Which of these four is your Friday? — owner / urgency / 30-day shape / outcome matrix
9. The 30-day shape — pick one pod, capture standard, shadow run, CRO readout
10. Under the hood + Two doors (Use it / Shape it)

Forbidden in this deck (until slide 10, and even then in plain English):
- "Execution Infrastructure Mandate", "AACE", "Cognitive Layer / Cognitive Infrastructure", "Context Gap Tax", "Semantic Debt", "Unified Rationale Log" as a labelled product term, "Adaptive Agentic Context Engine".
- Balance-scale or iceberg metaphors as openers. They read as seller-push.

Required: DRAFT and HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL badges on every slide (cover gets the diagonal watermark too). No em-dashes / en-dashes. White-theme presentation standard. GRN for outcomes, RED for risk.

Standing council for this deck: the five Tech DD seats + an **Insurance SME** (composite: former GCC carrier CRO, life/P&C reinsurance underwriter, ex-CBUAE compliance lead). Run before any edit.
