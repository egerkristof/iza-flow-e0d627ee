## Canonical thesis

LIZA is **The AI Governance Loop**: the continuous mechanism by which an organization safeguards what is most valuable to it AND scales what is best about it, at the speed AI now operates.

- Without it, **AI is dangerous** (standards drift, IP leaks, audit trail collapses).
- Without it, **AI is meaningless** (generic LLMs produce average output, no access to your judgment).

AI-native = governed. Ungoverned AI is not AI-native, it is AI-exposed.

## Required language

- **Category:** "The AI Governance Loop" (preferred). "Organizational AI Governance" acceptable as long-form.
- **Headline pair:** "Whatever you do not govern, AI invents. Whatever you do not safeguard, AI dissolves."
- **Wedge line (always pair with category):** "Model governance watches the AI. LIZA governs the moment of decision."
- **Promise line:** "Safeguard what is most valuable. Scale what is best about you. Continuously, at the speed of AI."
- "Loop" is non-negotiable. It defeats the audit-report / compliance-checkbox association of bare "governance."

## The 5 surfaces (memorize order)

| Surface | Safeguard | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Standards | Quality bar from drift and dilution | Consistent execution at AI velocity |
| Judgment | Senior expertise from attrition and averaging | Decisions that reflect your best people |
| Memory | Institutional knowledge from leaking into LLMs | Compounding context, owned not rented |
| Spend | AI budget from unanchored token consumption | Every token tied to a named outcome |
| Exposure | IP, audit trail, regulatory surface | Provable lineage for every AI decision |

Map to AACE primitives internally; do NOT lead with primitive names externally. Surfaces are the buyer-facing vocabulary.

## Competitive wedge

Credo AI, Fiddler, Arthur, Robust Intelligence, Holistic AI all use "AI governance" to mean model governance (monitor the LLM after it runs). LIZA means organizational governance (encode the org so the LLM serves it before it runs). Do not cede the word; qualify it.
