The €15k AI Opportunity Audit (`/offers/audit` and the matching PDF) is positioned as paid discovery for an inevitable partnership, not as a neutral diagnostic.

**Forbidden phrasing** (do not reintroduce):
- "product-agnostic"
- "LIZA OS is one option" / "never the conclusion"
- "vendor-neutral"
- "the audit stands alone, you are under no obligation"
- Any framing that lets the buyer take the roadmap to an internal team or another vendor without friction.

**Required stance:**
- We have a strong point of view: enforcement is the only thing that closes the Instruction Gap, and LIZA OS is the enforcement layer we built.
- The audit proves the diagnosis on the buyer's data; the deployment sprint (~€25k+) is the self-evident next step.
- "We are not here to give you options. We are here to prove the diagnosis on your data and architect the only solution we believe works."

**Why:** A neutral-auditor stance contradicts our category-creation thesis and lets buyers take €15k of analysis to a competitor. Strong conviction is more credible to the Head of AI persona than false objectivity.
