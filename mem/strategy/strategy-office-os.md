**Category:** Strategy Office OS. Cross-vertical FUNCTION (not industry). Pervasive across satcom, banking, pharma, federated holdings.

**Architecture role:** Bridge layer between Leadership Intent and Execution Expertise. NOT a third public layer in the architecture story; positioned as a function/use case sitting on top of the existing Liza stack.

**Lifecycle:** Scan → Synthesise → Route → Decide → Remember.

**ICP:** 3-6 person teams that must know everything fast without being expert on anything. They sit on top of fleet, finance, regulatory, engineering experts. Often perceived internally as one-way takers (ask, never give back).

**Real pain (per Fernando/Hispasat, May 8):** Not research time. Organisational latency. Expert teams take 2-8 days to answer because strategy team is deprioritised. No recurring workflow; every day is different (target eval Monday, competitor research Tuesday).

**Sales narrative:** "Become the team that walks in with answers, not questions." Reframe strategy team from asker to giver: every routed expert answer becomes a reusable asset other teams can pull. Strategy team becomes institutional memory of the company.

**Routes:**
- Function page: `/industries/strategy-office` (uses FunctionalLifecyclePage)
- Sales deck: `/strategy-office` (StrategyOfficeDeck.tsx, cloned from MADeck, investor slides removed)

**Forbidden:** Investment framing for this audience. They have no budget. Always sales deck.
