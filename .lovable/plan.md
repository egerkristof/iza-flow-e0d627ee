
# Plan: Lifecycle Investor Deck — Abstraction-First Rebuild

## Core Principle
Every slide presents a **universal infrastructure abstraction** the presenter can point at. Industry-specific color (Pharma, Professional Services, Financial Services) is layered verbally or via small callouts — never as the main structure.

## Industry Selection (Council Consensus)
| Industry | Role | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| **Pharma / Life Sciences** | Highest stakes example | Regulated, visceral, GMP failures resonate |
| **Professional Services** | Beachhead / deployed | Real proof, VC-relatable |
| **Financial Services** | Replaces Systems Engineering | Every VC understands it, compliance-heavy, judgment-intensive |
